🌸 Spring · March – May 🌡️ +8°C to +22°C

Blooming Steppe & Nauryz Renewal

Spring awakens the vast Kazakh steppe with wild tulips and irises. Across the country, yurts are raised for Nauryz Meiramy (March 21–23)—the ancient festival of the spring equinox—celebrated with Nauryz Kozhe, dombra concerts, and traditional horse games.

🎉 Key Holidays & Cultural Events

March 21–23: Nauryz Meiramy (Nomadic New Year)

The biggest cultural celebration of the year! Public squares fill with decorated yurts, cauldron-cooked Nauryz Kozhe, dombra competitions (aitys), and traditional swings (altybakan).

March marks the spring equinox. While northern steppes still have crisp mornings, southern cities like Turkistan and Shymkent burst into warm spring blossoms—making it the best month for cultural festivals.
